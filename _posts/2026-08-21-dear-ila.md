dear Ila,

you’re gentle and caring, thoughtful and true,
always putting someone else before you.

when someone needs you, you’re always around,
you’re the kind of person who won’t let them down.

you stand beside them when things aren’t okay,
and somehow you find the right things to say.

but I know you won’t ask for help when you’re low,
you’ll smile through the pain and never let it show.

you’ll say that you’re fine, though things may feel rough,
that’s just who you are you’ll say you’re strong enough.

and yes, I know you’re strong,
kind and humble, even when things go wrong.

but being strong doesn’t mean you can’t fall,
you don’t have to carry the weight of it all.

i know i don’t know you that well just yet,
there’s still so much about you we haven’t met.

but if something is hurting, if something’s unfair,
you can always tell me I promise I’ll care.

tell me your worries, whatever they may be,
tell me the things you don’t usually let people seee.

i might not have answers or know what to say,
but I’ll sit and listen, whatever the day.

i will listen to your stories, the good and the bad,
the moments that hurt and the ones that made you glad.

no judging, no questions, no need to pretend,
sometimes all you need is someone who’s a friend.

so don’t ever think that you’re walking alone,
or that every problem must be handled alone.

when the nights feel too long and the days feel wrong,
and when loneliness comes knocking at your door,

just remember,
you have someone to listen.

so keep being kind, keep being you,
keep caring for others like you always do.

you’re free to rest, you’re free to fall,
and if you ever need me, just give me a call.

i may not know your whole story just yet,
but I’m really glad that our paths have met.

you don’t have to explain, you don’t have to pretend,

just remember, 
you’ve got a friend.
