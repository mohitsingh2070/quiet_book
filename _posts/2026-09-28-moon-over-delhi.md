Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai ya ghutan ka saya<br>
Har saans mein zeher, yeh kisne banaya?<br>

<i>This isn't London fog, this is something deeper.</i><br>

Gale mein kharash, aankhon mein paani<br>
This isn't winter chill, yeh zahar hai Dilli wala<br>
AQI hits 500, flashing on the phone<br>
Hazardous red alert, can't even leave my home<br>
Construction ki dhool, gaadiyon ka shor<br>
Parali smoke, can't take it anymore<br>
Lungs feeling heavy, chest feeling tight<br>
Har saans ek jang hai everyday and every night<br>

Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai ya ghutan ka saya<br>
Har saans mein zeher, yeh kisne banaya?<br>

Yeah, we breathing in the gray, living day-to-day<br>
Welcome to the capital, choking on the sleigh<br>
Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai, feels like a gas chamber<br>
Politics be sleeping while we're in the danger<br>

<br>

Blame game on the TV, roz ka drama<br>
Wo state, yeh state, bas jhootha hangama<br>
Odd-even schemes and water cannons for a week<br>
But the problem's deep-rooted, the system is weak<br>

The rich got their purifiers, Swiss-tech imported<br>
Hum maask laga ke nikle, life feels so sordid<br>
They talk smart cities, bullet trains and mic<br>
What's the point of progress if you can't see the damn light?<br>

Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai ya ghutan ka saya<br>
Har saans mein zeher, yeh kisne banaya?<br>

Yeah, we breathing in the gray, living day-to-day<br>
Welcome to the capital, choking on the sleigh<br>
Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai, feels like a gas chamber<br>
Politics be sleeping while we're in the danger<br>

<br>

Chhote bachhe khanse, school ki chhutti<br>
Is this the price of progress? Yeh kaisi tarakki?<br>
We used to love the winter, the chai, the vibe<br>
Now it's just a countdown, trying to survive<br>

Survive, yeah, just tryna survive<br>

Yeh Delhi ki hawa hai ya ghutan ka saya<br>
Har saans mein zeher, yeh kisne banaya?<br>
