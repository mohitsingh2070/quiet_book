i feel most of us are *trapped*, the difference might be in the size and the nature of the cage. would have loved to share more on this, but anyways…

let me distract you with a story… (bedtime story)

once upon a time, there was a couple,
a husband and a wife.

who, like many other couples, would often quarrel with each other.
one day it crossed a certain threshold, so much so that the husband stormed out of the house. he could not bear it any longer; he was very much exasperated.

as he walked out, he was not sure where he was going.
then he found himself near a temple. it was a vishnu temple where he had never been before. so the story goes that there was nobody in the temple.

just the idol and him. though he was not a very religious person, lo and behold, vishnu himself appeared before him and told him, “ask me three boons and i will grant them.”

so he is very clever, so he asked that he will ask for only one for now, which he currently has in his mind.

vishnu said, “ask, what is it?”

he says, help me get rid of my wife.

vishnu again asked, “do you really want that?”

he says confidently, yes.

vishnu then said, “तथास्तु.”

now he goes back to his house and to his *pleasant* surprise, he saw packed bags waiting for him. his wife told him, “i am going.”

he again couldn’t believe his ears, so he enquired, “are you really going?” and she said yes.

he was happy to see her leave.

then he wanted to celebrate, so he calls his friends. his friends, after listening, told him, “what have you done? you should see our wives. your wife is such a noble woman compared to them, and on top of it she cooks very good food…” and they went on and on talking about her virtues.

he then realised that he had made a great mistake. so he asked his friends, “now, what to do?” his friends departed saying, “now we can’t even have good food in your house, so we are leaving.”

now he is again frustrated, and he finds himself meandering aimlessly, and he comes in front of the same temple. he goes inside the temple and again vishnu appeared before him and asks him, “are you ready for your second boon?”

he says yesssss (with a long face).

“i want my wife back.”

vishnu again asks him, “do you really want this?”

he confirms, and vishnu says, “तथास्तु.”

he then goes back to his home and to his *pleasant* surprise he sees all the luggage. and suddenly his wife prostrates before him and says, “forgive me for what i have done…” and he says, “don’t worry.”

when he goes in, he then confesses to her about the boons.
he tells her that there is one more boon left, and he asks her, “what should we ask for?”

they were confused, so they started asking their friends and relatives for advice. everyone says something. some say 100k is not enough, so ask for more… a lot of opinions like that. some tell him to ask for all the gold in the world, why only the world, ask for the gold in all the universe… some say, what’s the use if you die tomorrow? so ask for health and wealth.

he was very confused, but he was very clear of one thing that *nobody knew.*

and he went boldly to the temple, and again vishnu appeared before him, smiling as usual. vishnu asks him, “are you ready for your third boon?”

he says, “yes and no… will you permit me to ask a question before that?”

vishnu says, smiling, “please ask.”

then he says, “i am totally confused and i have lost faith in everybody except you, so tell me *what is it that i should ask of you that is in my best interest?*”

vishnu says, “you might not like the answer.”

he says, “i don’t care. i have reached the pits of my life and i know that you will show me the right direction.”

vishnu tells him, “ask that you will be contented with whatever you have.”

so he asked, and so he received.

the moment he came out of the temple, the world looked completely different. for the first time he noticed the green leaves on the trees, the moon in the sky, the clouds, and the wind around him.

it was always there, but he never noticed. the freshness of the air was remarkable.

when he reached his home, his wife didn’t look like the old wife. she was *beautiful*.

and there was a glow permeating from everything in the universe.

*nothing had changed, absolutely nothing had changed… in the outer realm, but everything had changed.*
