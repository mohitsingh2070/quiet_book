---
layout: post
title: "Dear Ila"
category: "poem"
subtitle: "For someone who is always there for everyone else."
---

<div class="poem">

<p>
Dear Ila,
</p>

<p>
you’re gentle and caring, thoughtful and true,<br>
always putting someone else before you.
</p>

<p>
when someone needs you, you’re always around,<br>
you’re the kind of person who won’t let them down.
</p>

<p>
you stand beside them when things aren’t okay,<br>
and somehow you find the right things to say.
</p>

<p>
but I know you won’t ask for help when you’re low,<br>
you’ll smile through the pain and never let it show.
</p>

<p>
you’ll say that you’re fine, though things may feel rough,<br>
that’s just who you are — you’ll say you’re strong enough.
</p>

<p>
and yes, I know you’re strong,<br>
kind and humble, even when things go wrong.
</p>

<p>
but being strong doesn’t mean you can’t fall,<br>
you don’t have to carry the weight of it all.
</p>

<p>
i know i don’t know you that well just yet,<br>
there’s still so much about you we haven’t met.
</p>

<p>
but if something is hurting, if something’s unfair,<br>
you can always tell me — I promise I’ll care.
</p>

<p>
tell me your worries, whatever they may be,<br>
tell me the things you don’t usually let people see.
</p>

<p>
i might not have answers or know what to say,<br>
but I’ll sit and listen, whatever the day.
</p>

<p>
i will listen to your stories, the good and the bad,<br>
the moments that hurt and the ones that made you glad.
</p>

<p>
no judging, no questions, no need to pretend,<br>
sometimes all you need is someone who’s a friend.
</p>

<p>
so don’t ever think that you’re walking alone,<br>
or that every problem must be handled alone.
</p>

<p>
when the nights feel too long and the days feel wrong,<br>
and when loneliness comes knocking at your door,
</p>

<p>
just remember,<br>
you have someone to listen.
</p>

<p>
so keep being kind, keep being you,<br>
keep caring for others like you always do.
</p>

<p>
you’re free to rest, you’re free to fall,<br>
and if you ever need me, just give me a call.
</p>

<p>
i may not know your whole story just yet,<br>
but I’m really glad that our paths have met.
</p>

<p>
you don’t have to explain, you don’t have to pretend,
</p>

<p>
just remember,<br>
you’ve got a friend.
</p>

</div>
