last year when i was leaving my home for bangalore i just wrote this at the airport, it's a bit close to my heart.

you are yourself when you’re home 
no pretenses, no masks to wear. 
no need to hide, no need to perform,
you can be calm, you can be wild 
even if you’re a storm, it’s fine. 
life moves slow, wrapped in a gentle warmth. 

you fight for a cause, or cause a fight. 
you carry sorrow, you savor joy. 
you own your mistakes in the comfort of your room. 
you play, you cry, you dream, you try. 

you look up to your mother, a little less to your father, 
and to everyone else, with wide, wondering eyes. 
you leave for school with plans for what you'll do after, 
only to come home, later, boasting how 
food outside can't match what’s made here. 

you grow up. you grow out. 
and never realize your time at home was always on lease. 

you see the allure, the dazzling world 
you dive deep, you drown fast, 
and feel the weight of depth (just a metaphor, of course). 

then, one day, you return - just for a visit. 
you find stories scattered across the silence, 
and wish to gather them again 
but time runs too thin to restore it all. 

you spot things no one uses anymore. 
you can’t throw them out, 
but you can't carry them either. 

the old camera. the dusty frames. 
the radio. the cassettes. 
nothing works, yet everything awakens a memory. 

So you sit. and you write. for a while. 

"i’d love to stay," you whisper, 
"but i must leave - again - for a while." 

you try to capture everything in that final glance, 
but no one you grew up with remains
neither people nor the things that once breathed life. 

only two familiar faces remain 
weathered, wrinkled, waiting, waving, 
hoping your next stay brings just one more day. 

you promise to return. 
but deep down, you know 
life is short, and fast. 
and now...
