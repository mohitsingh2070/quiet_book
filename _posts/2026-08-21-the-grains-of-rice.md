title: "The Grains of Rice"
category: "thought"
subtitle: "On roles, circumstance, and playing the hand we are given."
---

Sometimes the weight of a situation becomes unbearable.

One wants to lock oneself in a room,
cut off from everything and everybody else.

But perhaps we need not respond from our lower nature,
nor react mechanically like a machine when a button is pressed.

The way forward depends on the choice we make
within the circumstance we are given.

A grain of rice might believe
that it possesses the kinetic energy
with which it is jumping up and down.

But we know the source beneath it—
the kindled fire—
is what makes the movement possible.

Suppose you are acting in a drama.

You know you are playing a character.
You are neither the king nor the pauper.

You are simply acting,
playing along.

Perhaps this is the way life should be lived:

play with joy,
enjoy whatever happens,
and act with full freedom and skill,
knowing that you are neither the king nor the pauper.

*You cannot get the best of roles every time.*

But you can play
as if you were destined to play the role you received.

Whatever cards you are dealt,
play them fully.

One who loses a kingdom in a drama
knows very well that it was never truly his.

Perhaps that is also how one learns
to lose gracefully.

We are all like grains of rice,
moving up and down in life.

Some are unconscious.
Some are aware that the roles are temporary.

Knowing that not everyone understands
the source of this movement,
perhaps we can be a little more compassionate.
