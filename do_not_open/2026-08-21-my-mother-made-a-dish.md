my mother made a dish.
i said, not this sweet.
another day she cooked something else.
i said, too dull, too pale.
some evenings she tried again.
i said the hotel makes it better.
she would just look at me, maybe tired, maybe quiet.

now i am a thousand miles away.
i have seen the world. tasted everything.
and i eat only to survive.
no complaints now.
just food that fills the stomach,
never the heart.

i want to go back this time
not to eat,
but to say sorry
for every small sentence i threw carelessly.

recently i have started telling lies.
to friends. to you.
“i’m fine.”
“best day ever.”
“i’m happy here.”
even “i’ll always be there.”
as if distance has not already shown
how small i can become.

i remember when even a scratch on my hand
brought tears to your eyes.
your one gentle blow
used to cure everything.
now i get hurt
and pain just stays.

even today when i tear up,
i remember you.
every solution i ever needed
used to sit in your lap.
now i search in the dark
for something that feels like you.

i used to overeat your food
until i couldn’t move.
now i forget to eat.
or maybe i forgot the taste of being cared for.

i am your part.
your story you tell with pride.
and i am here
learning that the safest place i ever knew
was your aanchal.
i held it tightly and slept,
not knowing safety can end.

without you
the world feels too big
and useless at the same time.
what is the point of seeing everything
if the one place that felt like home
is not near?

maybe we need distance
to understand value.
maybe love has to be missed
before it is understood.

but some understandings
arrive too late,
and too deep.

why do you love me this much…
even when i did not know
how to love you back.
