I’ve got a pair of bullocks, a plough and a little field,
Seeds that will turn to gold one day,
I’ve got my God above, and a good girl who loves me,
I’m the richest man in the world!

Thank You, thank You, Lord above, for smiling down on me,
I’m richer than a king could ever hope to be.
Health and love and happiness are treasures sweet and free,
The richest man in all creation surely envies me!

I’ve got a pair of bullocks, a plough and a little field,
Seeds that will turn to gold one day,
I’ve got my God above, and a good girl who loves me,
I’m the richest man in the world!

There is water in my well, and peace within my heart,
A faithful wife who stands by me, even when apart.
Enough to watch a movie and a meal outside sometimes,
A roof, a bed, a fishing rod, and friends for all my life.

I’ve got a pair of bullocks, a plough and a little field,
Seeds that will turn to gold one day,
I’ve got my God above, and a good girl who loves me,
I’m the richest man in the world!

I may not have much money, nor riches piled up tall,
But tell me, what is wealth if there’s no joy in it at all?
Some may think I’m poor indeed, but I know that isn’t true,
For when I count my blessings, I’m the richest that I knew!

I’ve got a pair of bullocks, a plough and a little field,
Seeds that will turn to gold one day,
I’ve got my God above, and a good girl who loves me,
I’m the richest man in the world!

Now the wealthy boast about the fancy food they eat,
But I can surely match them and perhaps even compete!
I’ve got fields of golden sugarcane,
And mango trees after every rain.
Don’t talk to me of foreign caviar,
It’s only fish eggs in a costly jar!
I’ve got a village pond so wide,
With fresh fish swimming side by side.
No fancy swimming pool for me,
The river by the banyan tree!
I’m a lucky man! I’m a lucky soul!
I’m wealthy! I’m wealthy!

And you, my dear, with your heart of gold,
Are richer than treasures ever sold.
With God above and love so true,
The richest woman is surely you!
