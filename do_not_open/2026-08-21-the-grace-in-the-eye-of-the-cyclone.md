title: "The Grace in the Eye of the Cyclone"

I was thinking about Sita in the forest.

She hears the voice of Maricha,
disguised as Rama's faint and wailing cry.

She becomes agitated, worried, frightened.
On top of that, she quarrels with Lakshmana
and asks him to go and help him.

And honestly, the thought itself
that someone whom you love deeply might be in danger,
might be at the verge of his life somewhere far away in the forest—

that thought alone can shake a person completely.

And this is all happening while she herself
is already going through a phase of life
she perhaps never had even imagined for herself.

Exile.
Uncertainty.
Discomfort.
Fear.

Everything served together.

Yet what amazes me is this:

in the midst of all that emotional chaos,
when the bhikshuk comes there,
she still has the calmness and clarity of mind
to serve the athithi.

Somehow,
she still remembered and practiced

*the grace in the eye of the cyclone.*
