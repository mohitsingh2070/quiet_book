It's an embarrassing bookshop story.

I went to this shop - it was a bit far from my house, to see if I could find some interesting bits on history (any classic). It was around 8 o'clock, and I guess most people had gone for dinner, so the shop was almost empty except for one girl who was sitting there with her headphones on.

Now, I was distracted, but soon I came back to my original quest. I have no idea why I started to behave *strangely*. I started ignoring her. Then again, just in the flow, I glanced at her (I know it's creepy), but she was also looking at me!! Interestingly, her headphones were off (oh!! I could see her ears, that means she wants to talk to me... Hmmmm).

Now I completely forgot what I was supposed to do, and my acting skills are abysmally low. (The book titles on the stack were also weird - *The Outcomes*.) Then I again went back in search of the book (*mai to kitab dekhne aaya hu*).

Then, after a few seconds, she came near me and asked, "What are you looking for?" I guess my acting was *sooo* weird that she felt the need to help! Then she asked if I was from around here. I told her no, I live in the town. She then said, "Wow, you travelled this far for books." I told her that once in a while I come to this place and... (don't exactly remember what I said after that).

She mentioned that she lives nearby, but she didn't know about this place and had come across this store only very recently. Then she asked me if I could help her find a book for her friend. I said sure (but in my head this question had created a tornado of thoughts). It's like a day-to-day thing, recommending a book... GREAT.

She asked me how often I come here. Meanwhile, my friend, who was standing outside (he is not a book lover), texted me, "Dinner ke liye bhi puch hi lo" (sarcasm).

I, being me, started talking about the book, how this is one of the best books for this, this reason... *ad infinitum* and *ad nauseam*. Then she asked what my plans were after that. I told her I would go back after hopping through a few more shops.

Then I left in the middle of my conversation to go back to my books (judge me as much as you can). She then left.

I was very shy, so I was in tension about what to do, how to approach. Then I had this *revelation*,what had I just done? I could have asked for her number in that moment. By the time I went downstairs, she had already left.

And most stupidly, I went to the bookstore guy and asked him if he could give me her contact info because I needed to give her some information about the book we had been talking about. I felt so foolish the moment I said it. The guy said, "No, we can't give that." Then I gave my number to him and asked that if she ever came back, he could give it to her.

The next two days, I was just in analytical mode, thinking that I could have done this, could have done that, etc. But at that time, the mental blockade was real.
