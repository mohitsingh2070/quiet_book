title: "In the Cradle of Cosmic Uncertainties"
category: "poem"
subtitle: "An early fragment."
---

In the cradle of cosmic uncertainties
I try fathoming the connotation of life

Truth to be told,
I lie beneath the abyss of infinite

Mere thinking about the tenebrous womb
from which I arrive
puts the mind in the voids of demise

Being aware in the kingdom of trivial life
Always feeling forlorned in the looping time

Montaging the odyssey of formless divine

A feeling of longing enigmatic to define

Talk to me
to end my ephemeral disguise.
