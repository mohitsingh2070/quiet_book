---

title: "The Moon and You"
category: "poem"
subtitle: "A small observation from tonight."
---

<div class="poem-entry">

  <p class="poem-intro">
    Some thoughts are better left small.
  </p>

  <figure class="poem-image">
    <img src="{{ '/assets/images/Poem_moon_and_you.jpeg' | relative_url }}"
         alt="A poem about the moon and someone dear">
  </figure>

  <div class="poem-note">
    <p>
      I think the moon was doing perfectly well tonight.
      Calm. Quiet. Bright.
    </p>

    <p>
      Then I looked at you (in my imagination !).
    </p>

    <p>
      And somehow, the moon seemed a little offended.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="poem-closing">
    <p>
      That's all for today.
    </p>
  </div>

</div>
