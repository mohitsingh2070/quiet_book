most people are not consciously selfish.
they are just absorbed by their fears of uncertainty, roles they are expected to perform, weighed down by imaginary duties they must uphold, anxieties from societal expectations placed upon them, and deep seated conditioning. 
they are mostly looking at life from the narrow window of what feels urgent and necessary for them in that moment.

they are acting from the play of the _gunas_, not from malice.

when someone doesn’t ask "how are you?", it often isn’t because you don’t matter, but because they themselves are not awake enough to see beyond their own momentum.

why does it hurt so much then?
because one is aware.
awareness creates sensitivity and sensitivity feels everything more sharply, like about being taken for granted

people take for granted what feels _stable_ and does not demand attention.

at times this weight becomes unbearable, and one feels to just lock oneself in a room and wanting to cut off from everything and everybody else. even the well wishers. you don't want to see anybody's face  _(unless the other person is in the same state)_. reasons might be many from say you want something badly but you don't get it and even worse others get what you long for)

so how to come out of this abysmal degenerative state.

here one need not respond from one's lower nature nor react mechanically like a  machine when button is pressed. 
the way forward is based on the choice of circumstance one is in.

like grain of rice believes that it has the kinetic energy through which it is jumping up and down but we know it is the source beneath (the kindled fire) that is the real source. suppose you are acting in a drama. you know you are just playing a character, you are neither the king nor the pauper. you are just acting and playing along. this is the way a life should be lived. you play with joy and enjoy whatever happens. with full freedom and skill you act knowing fully that you are neither the king nor the pauper.

*you can not get the best of roles every time.*
but you play as if you are destined to play that role. whatever cards you are dealt with just play the fullest with what you have. one should never be despondent even if one loses, like in a drama, the one who loses a kingdom knows it very well that it was never truly his in the first place, he teaches how to lose gracefully.

we are all like grains of rice, moving up and down in life. some are unconscious and some are aware that the roles are temporary. 
knowing that not everyone understands the source of this movement or the play of the _gunas_ (satvic, rajasic and tamsic), we need to be compassionate towards them.
