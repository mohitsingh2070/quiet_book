help me write this poem on mangoes (four lines each) tune it (rephrase, choose best of words suitable)-

i dont have to even pen down which itself is insufficient even if i take the whole of sky as canvas and all 7 oceans as the ink, that itself would be 1/16 of the appearance of yours, how can i even describe your sweetness and glories.

but still i engage in this self defeatng endevour not because i doubt my capabalities but because it forces me to dwell on you and your form and the memory i have and in the stories i cherish and fantasies i enjoy.

it wold be grave injustice if i start with your qualities, i should at least start whre you give qualities to others, i will be blessed if i am even able to reach this to your qualities. In the epic of bharata, the Ramayana , rama is himself not complete without your nama.

you give royality to ayodhya by mere presence of yours the idea of prosperity beauty and richness cant be even described. without you the balakanda and ayodhya kanda would be dry and without glamour.

In aranya kanda you were the one that gave rama relief by the mesmerising view of yours near pampa lake.  you took the anguish of rama and gave him serene peace.

you were the one for whose arrival even rama waited for four months, your falling itself gave solace to the devotees and only after your dissappearance rama became impatience and lakshmana became angered.

in ravaana palace what gold cant achieve was achieved by your golden colors, the richness was not of the buildings but of the gardens where you royally reside and give the sensual richness of paradise.

your sacrifice in the yoddha in fullfilling the entire army is missed by even great tellers,  you were the one who witnessed the whole of ramayana , in samskrit ayanna means a journey, here along with rama its was you who accompanied him in his highs and lows. The start of balakanda is your journey of transformation of  flower to the raw mangoes ( ayodhya kanda ) some raw mangoes are plukked and taken to ripen at different place (just like rama was taken to forest), the hardships (aranya) make you mature and removs the sourness, you ready to meet your fragrance and sweetness(sugrivan and hanuman) ) then you announce your arrival of perfection through color and smell like the hanumans announcement of yours in lanka.
