title: "A Sweet, Gloomy Glory"
category: "fragment"
subtitle: "A collection of images that never quite became a poem."
---

A sweet, gloomy glory of despair.

Like a honey-dipped arrow—
sweet at the tip,
fatal underneath,
and somehow still beautiful enough
to aim at your own chest again.

Like a matchstick
born knowing its brightest moment
is also its death.

Like train tracks
that spend lifetimes beside each other
yet never meet.

Like a comet:
beautiful only because it is already leaving.

Like a snowball
you know, if you keep it,
will die.

The grief becomes the harvest.

The temperature grows colder,
the leaves become frost,
a new cycle begins
and the old colours are lost.
