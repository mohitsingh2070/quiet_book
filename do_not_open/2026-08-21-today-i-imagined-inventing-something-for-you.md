today i imagined inventing something for you 
*the perfect working machine.*

at least .... this is what i promise....

just put your chores inside,
drop a coin,
turn the switch,
wait ten seconds… time,
everything done quicker than a blink.

laundry?
it folded the clothes…
but somehow turned three socks into five
and none of them matched.

dinner?
it tried making dosa…
it made something round, philosophical, and slightly confused.
and served *stories for dinner*
and laughter for dessert.

cleaning the house?
it cleaned the floor, the shelves…
and relicated the dust under places we may never discover again,
and somehow also cleaned *your unnecessary worries*.

arranging the room?
it arranged everything perfectly…
except the one thing everyone loses 
our sense of enough.

for a moment i thought 
ah, life would be perfect with such a machine.

but clearly… even my imaginary machine
cannot keep life perfectly arranged.
maybe life was never meant to be perfect,
only *beautifully imperfect.*

because if every chore vanished in ten seconds,
we might also lose the small things,
the stories we complain about today
but laugh at tomorrow.

so here is the only birthday wisdom i have #

*learn to love what you have today,*
before life teaches you
how to love what you have lost.

and today 
i hope you keep a little time
for yourself too.

after all,
even the best machines deserve
a day off.
