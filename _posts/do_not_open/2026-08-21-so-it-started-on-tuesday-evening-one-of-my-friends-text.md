so it started on tuesday evening, one of my friends texted me that he needs some money so if I can send him. I did. He was a friend with whom I used to go and play basketball in the morning after running in the morning. 

To tell a bit about him, he was the most charming guy in our group. He used to teach yoga and along with it he later joined as a gym trainer. (he met with an accident from bike and had plates and rods inserted in his thighs and it took him 2.5 years to walk again). I used to meet him and try motivating him as he was going through a lot. He was not worried about himself but his mother who was suffering because of him. He used to tell me that in this age i should be the one that should care for her but fate has turned everything upside down. The same month he met with the accident, he got into the masters program in some Sports Authority of India college, which he had to forego.


He suddenly called me and thanked me for sending the money (I guess he might have asked from others as well) and he said to me that I was the only one who didn't even ask for the reason before sending it.


Now he asked about my things and in the casual conversation he said something that shook me to the core. He asked me how my writing was going.
I told him it's going side by side along with my PhD.

He then casually said, "aise hi side se karte raho, PhD me, phir job karoge to side se karna, shadi wagerah ke bad side se karna, settle hoge tab bhi side se karna, phir mar jaoge to ye side me reh jayega". And then he cut the call after some time.
