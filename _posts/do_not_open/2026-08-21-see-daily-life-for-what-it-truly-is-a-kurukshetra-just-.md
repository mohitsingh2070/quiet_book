see daily life for what it truly is - a kurukshetra. just as described in the very first sloka of the gita, life itself is a battlefield. even arjuna one of the greatest warriors stood trembling and confused in the middle of that field.

if he could waver, what about ordinary mortals like you and me?

notice where the gita begins- not in a palace, not in comfort, not in a quiet corner of contemplation, but right in the heart of a battlefield - a place where clarity is hardest to find, where the mind collapses under pressure, where even action feels impossible. arjuna couldn’t think, couldn’t act, couldn’t even process what was happening.

that is exactly how life feels for so many of us today. (context might be modern but essence is the same)

so what should one do?

the gita gives a simple, timeless answer-
approach someone who is not confused.
someone calm. someone genuine. someone whose heart is steady, who is not trapped in their own dilemmas.

arjuna went to the best guide he could possibly find - krishna himself.
